# V60 technique

## Measurements

- Mug: 24g coffee, 400g water
- Travel mug: 30g coffee, 480g water
- Jug: 2 x 27g Coffee, 450g water

## Method

1. Grind desired amount of coffee @ 60
2. Rinse filter paper
3. Put coffee in filter cone and shake to flatten
4. Put receptacle on scale and reset it
5. Swirl in water until scale reads twice the weight of the coffee
6. Let sit for 30 seconds
7. Swirl in water to max over a minute
8. Stir the coffee a little bit in each direction
9. Give the V60 a wee swish
10. Let it drain
