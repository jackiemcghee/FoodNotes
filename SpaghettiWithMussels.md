# Spaghetti with mussels

## Ingredients

- [ ] 200g pasta
- [ ] 500g mussels
- [ ] Garlic
- [ ] 3 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] Dried chilli flakes
- [ ] Juice of half a lemon
- [ ] 2 handfuls of flat leaf parsley

## Method

1. Cook pasta until al dente
2. Steam the mussels
3. Fry garlic and chilli flakes until garlic is tender
4. Add the reserved mussel liquid and simmer until you have a thin sauce
5. Add drained pasta and mussels to sauce and stir
6. Add salt, emon juice and parsley then stir and serve
