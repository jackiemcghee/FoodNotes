# Spicy friend potatoes

## Ingredients

- 1kg potatoes
- 5 cloves of garlic
- 1 onion
- 1 tablespoon of cumin
- 1 tablespoon of chili flakes
- 2 tablespoons of chili oil
- 1 teaspoon of five spice
- 1 teaspoon of salt
- t tablespoon of toasted sesame seeds
- 1 cyboe

## Method

1. Peel and cube potatoes (2cm) then steam for 10 -- 12 minutes
2. Heat oil (medium high) and add potatoes. Fry until golden and starting to crisp
3. Add garlic and onion and stir for about a minute
4. Add chili oil and stir for about a minute
5. Add salt and five spice and stir for about a minute
6. Add sesame seeds and cyboe, mix well and serve
