# Cold sesame noodles

## Ingredients

### Noodles

- [ ] 340g egg noodles
- [ ] 2 tbsp ground nut oil
- [ ] 2 tsp minced garlic
- [ ] 2 tsp grated gnger
- [ ] 2 tsp sesame seeds
- [ ] 1 cucumber, julienned
- [ ] 2 carrots, julienned
- [ ] 2 cyboes, green shoots, thinly sliced

### Sauce

- [ ] 3 tbsp tahini
- [ ] 1 tsp sesame oil
- [ ] 2 tbsp peanut butter
- [ ] 2 tbsp soy sauce
- [ ] 2 tsp chilli paste
- [ ] 0.5 tsp cayenne pepper or sze chuan pepper
- [ ] 1 tbsp sesame oil
- [ ] 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- [ ] 3 tbsp water

## Method

1. Cook noodles to minimum time, drain and rinse with cold water. Toss with 1tbps ground nut oil
2. Heat 1tbsp ground nut oil over a medium heat. Add the garlic and the ginger and gently cook until fragrant. Remove from heat and set aside
3. In a medium bowl, add all the sauce ingredients and gently whisk until smooth. Fold in the cooked ginger and garlic.
4. Pour the sauce over the noodles, add the cucumber and the carrot and toss
5. Serve with sesame seeds and cyboes sprinlled on top.
