# Waffles

## Ingredients

- [ ] 4 eggs, separated
- [ ] 1 cup flour
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon salt
- [ ] 3 tablespoons butter, melted
- [ ] 1 cup milk

## Method

1. Beat egg yolks until light and fluffy.
2. Add vanilla and butter, mixing until combined.
3. In separate bowl, mix together flour and salt.
4. Add to egg mixture, alternating with milk, until well combined. 
5. Beat egg whites until stiff and fold into batter.
