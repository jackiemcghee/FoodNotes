# Easy red lentil dahl

## Ingredients

- 250g red lentils
- 750ml water
- Tin of chopped tomatoes
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1 teaspoon salt

## Method

1. Cook lentils (lid on) in water for around 10 minutes until they just about start to break down
2. Strain lentils and put back in the pan
3. Add tomatoes
4. Add garam masala and salt and cook for 5 minutes (and can simmer at a low heat for a further 20 minutes or so)

OPTIONAL: can wilt some spinach in prior to serving
