# Bacon turnovers

## Ingredients

- [ ] 8 rashers of bacon
- [ ] 120g cheddar cheese
- [ ] Puff pastry
- [ ] 1 egg

## Method

1. Pre-heat over to 210
2. Cook bacon and leave to the side
3. Grate cheese
4. Cut puff pastry into four slices
5. Assemble turnovers
6. Brush with egg
7. Bake for 15 minutes
