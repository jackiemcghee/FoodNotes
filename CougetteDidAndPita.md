# Courgette dip with pita

## Ingrdients

- [ ] 2 large courgettes
- [ ] 3 cloves of garlic, minced
- [ ] 1.5 tbsp EVOO
- [ ] 2 tbsp tahini
- [ ] 1.5 tbsp lemon juice
- [ ] 1 tsp grated lemon zest
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/5 tsp black pepper
- [ ] EVOO and freshly chopped oregano, dill, basil to serve

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil.
2. Add courgettes and reduce to a simmer.
3. Cook until courgettes are fork-tender (20 -- 25 minutes).
4. Transfer to a colander to drain until cool to the touch (30 minute).
5. Mince courgettes in potato ricer. 
6. Place back in the colander and let drain 10-15 more minutes.
7. Transfer to a large bowl.
8. To the bowl, add garlic, tahini, olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, salt and pepper; stir until combined.
9. Transfer to a serving bowl. Top with a drizzle of olive oil and fresh herbs, if desired.
10. Toast pita and serve
