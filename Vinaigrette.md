# Vinaigrette

## French-style

- [ ] 3 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 tbsp Vinegar
- [ ] 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 1/8 tsp salt

## Burger-style

- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 tbsp vinegar
- [ ] 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 1/2 tsp honey
- [ ] Salt & plenty of black pepper
- [ ] Splash of pickle juice
